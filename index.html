🚀 Elon Musk

Visionary, Entrepreneur, Innovator
by Walusimbi Leon (SGSS)

About

Elon Musk is the richest person on Earth — a visionary entrepreneur who has revolutionized multiple industries simultaneously. From electric cars to reusable rockets, from social media to artificial intelligence, Musk's impact on technology and humanity is unmatched in the modern era.

This book traces his extraordinary journey from a bullied child in South Africa to the most influential businessman of the 21st century. It covers his early ventures, his bold bets on SpaceX and Tesla, his controversial acquisition of Twitter, his role in government efficiency, and his unrelenting vision for humanity's future among the stars.
